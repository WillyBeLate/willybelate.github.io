Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

WillyBeLate Pipeline (the "App") is a personal desktop tool developed and used by the content creator WillyBeLate, who is responsible for the data it processes.

2. What data the App processes

When you connect your TikTok account, the App receives and stores:

  • Your TikTok open ID and display name, to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token and a refresh token, so the App can upload drafts on your behalf.
  • The granted permissions (user.info.basic, video.upload).
  • A log of uploads: file name, upload time, TikTok's processing status and the caption you entered.

The App also reads the video files you choose to process. It does not access your contacts, messages, followers, location or any other TikTok data.

3. Where the data is stored

All data stays on your own computer, in a local database inside the App's folder. There is no server, no cloud storage and no analytics or tracking.

4. Who receives the data

Video files and the necessary authorization data are sent only to TikTok, when you start an upload. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

5. How long the data is kept

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the App or they expire. Upload logs are kept until you delete them. Disconnecting deletes the tokens and revokes the App's access at TikTok.

6. Your rights

You can disconnect the App at any time with the command pipeline tiktok logout or in your TikTok settings. Because all data is stored locally, you can view or delete it directly on your computer. Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict processing of your data.
